1. Overview & Core Premise

Welcome to WeatherSnap, an Android meteorological application developed by KAVTORS. WeatherSnap combines live weather forecasts with real-time photographic ground-truth evidence. Users can check current atmospheric conditions, capture verified weather photos, save records locally on their device, and optionally publish real-time reports to the shared community feed.

We value transparency and user privacy. This policy explains what information is collected, how it is processed and stored, where it is shared, and how you retain control over your data.

2. Information We Process & Collect

A. Weather Queries & Location Metadata

WeatherSnap does not request, track, or access your device's continuous GPS or background fine location.

  • City Searches: When you search for a city, your search term is sent to the Open-Meteo Geocoding API to look up coordinates (latitude and longitude) and place names.
  • Forecast Retrieval: WeatherSnap queries Open-Meteo for real-time atmospheric data (temperature, humidity, wind speed, pressure, and weather conditions) using the city's coordinates.
  • Default Location: If you select a city as your default home location, its identifier, name, and coordinates are saved locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences for quick retrieval on launch.

B. Device Camera & Photographic Evidence

WeatherSnap requires access to your device camera (android.permission.CAMERA) solely for:

  • Taking live, real-time ground-truth weather photos using the in-app CameraX viewfinder.
  • Attaching photographic evidence to your local saved reports or community weather submissions.

Camera access occurs only while actively using the camera screen. WeatherSnap never accesses your camera in the background or captures photos without your explicit tap on the shutter button.

C. Community Weather Reports (User-Generated Content)

When you choose to publish a weather report to the Community Reports feed, the following information is submitted to our cloud backend:

  • Weather Photograph: Compressed photo evidence captured via CameraX.
  • Meteorological Observations: Observed temperature, condition description, relative humidity, wind speed, and atmospheric pressure.
  • City Identification: City name, administrative region (state/province), country name, geographic coordinates, and Open-Meteo city ID.
  • Field Notes: Any optional text notes (up to 1,000 characters) you submit alongside the report.
  • Report Timestamp: The exact date and time the report was submitted.
  • Author Identifier: An opaque anonymous user UUID assigned by the backend (detailed below).
Public Visibility of Community Submissions Community reports and photographs published to the community feed are publicly accessible to all users of the app and viewable via public image links. Do not include private personal data, recognizable faces, confidential documents, or sensitive private property in your photographs or field notes.

D. Anonymous Authentication & Identification

WeatherSnap features anonymous cloud authentication powered by Supabase.

  • The application does not present a traditional account registration, email/password signup, or phone number login form.
  • Upon first interacting with cloud services, the app securely initializes an anonymous session via Supabase Auth and receives an opaque UUID (such as a1b2c3d4-e5f6-...).
  • This anonymous session token is persisted locally on your device in private app storage (weathersnap_supabase_auth).
  • Purpose: This identifier exists strictly to allow the app to label your own submissions as "YOUR REPORT", protect backend Row-Level Security (RLS) policies, and grant you exclusive authority to delete your own published reports. It is not connected to your legal name, email, contact list, or hardware identifiers.

E. Local Saved Reports (Offline Room Database)

If you save a report locally instead of (or in addition to) publishing it, the report data and photo are stored strictly on your device inside an offline SQLite database via Android Room. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly publish it to the community.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

WeatherSnap integrates minimal, trusted third-party services to perform core weather and cloud synchronization functionality:

Provider Service / Role Information Received Privacy Information
Open-Meteo GmbH Weather forecast and city geocoding API Queried city names and city center geographic coordinates. No personal identity or API tokens transmitted. Open-Meteo Terms & Privacy

Advertising & Tracking Notice: WeatherSnap does not contain advertising SDKs (such as Google AdMob) and does not contain behavioral analytics or third-party tracking software (such as Facebook Pixel or commercial data broker trackers).

4. Data Retention, Storage & Deletion Rights

A. How to Delete Your Community Reports

You retain full ownership and control over your published community submissions:

  • Open the My Reports screen in the WeatherSnap app.
  • Locate the report you wish to remove and tap the Delete icon.
  • Deletion is immediate: the backend database record is removed, and the associated photo file in cloud storage is permanently erased.

B. Important Limitations Regarding Anonymous Sessions

Notice on Clearing App Storage & Uninstallation: Because WeatherSnap uses anonymous sessions rather than email-based accounts, your ownership credentials are saved exclusively within the app's local storage sandbox.

If you clear the application's storage data or uninstall the app before deleting your published reports, your anonymous ownership token will be wiped from your device. You will no longer be recognized as the author of those previous reports.

C. Manual Deletion Requests

If you lose access to your device, clear your data, or need assistance removing any previously published report or photo from the community feed, you may request manual deletion at any time:

  1. Send an email to kunal.connect7@gmail.com with the subject "WeatherSnap Data Deletion Request".
  2. Provide the approximate date, city name, and a description or thumbnail of the photo you wish to remove.
  3. Our team will verify the content and permanently purge the record and image file from our database and cloud storage within 48 hours.

D. Local Data Retention

Local reports and local cached files saved via Room or SharedPreferences remain exclusively on your device. You can clear them anytime through Android's system settings (Settings > Apps > WeatherSnap > Storage > Clear Data), or by uninstalling the application.

5. Security of Your Information

We implement security best practices across our backend architecture:

  • All network communication between the app, Open-Meteo, and Supabase is encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS and WSS).
  • The cloud database enforces PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (RLS) policies. Only the authenticated author matching the cryptographic session token can delete their reports.
  • Storage uploads are strictly constrained to unique, isolated folder paths matching the authenticated author's identifier, preventing unauthorized modifications.

6. Children's Privacy

WeatherSnap does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has submitted personal information or photographic content through our community features, please contact us immediately for prompt deletion.
